    About FitFuel 🔥

    Calorie tracking + workout planning — simple, honest, free.

    What is FitFuel?

    FitFuel ek calorie aur workout tracker hai jahan aap apni daily food log kar sakte hain (veg, non-veg, healthy, junk — sab), nutrients (vitamins, minerals, omega aur bahut kuch) dekh sakte hain, workout library se exercise seekh sakte hain, apna personalised workout plan bana sakte hain, aur progress tracking page par consistency dekh sakte hain. Aapka data ek real database me save hota hai — login karne ke baad dubara details bharne ki zaroorat nahi.

    Nutrition values rough estimates hain — doctor/dietitian se baat karke hi diet badlein.
